# Montserrat — source files required
Font binaries are **not included** in this handoff. Download official Montserrat font files from:
https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/montserrat
https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Montserrat

Web: download Montserrat WOFF2 for weights 400, 500, 600, 700, 800 and place under your application's public/fonts/ directory.
Mobile: download official Montserrat TTF/OTF (or convert from the official source where licensing permits) and register weights in the mobile project.
Keep the upstream OFL license with the downloaded font files.
Do not use Inter: the design-system typography is Montserrat.
