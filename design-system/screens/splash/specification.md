# Mobile Splash

Status: design in progress; approved decisions below. Not implementation-ready.

## Confirmed

- Mobile startup surface. Use the exact [primary Smashi mark](../../../docs/BRAND_LOGO.md), not a crop from the mockup.
- Slogan: `PLAY • CONNECT • GROW`.
- Entire screen is static: no entrance, fades, movement, scale changes, or animated background. Do not add a presentation delay.
- Smashi web and mobile use one fixed visual theme, independent of system appearance. This supersedes older Light/Dark/System design guidance. Consumer migration details are pending.
- Use only the six approved brand-palette colors as design inputs; do not sample new colors from mockups.

## Approved backdrop — `splash-backdrop-r1`

Approved by the product owner on 2026-11-20. Use the backdrop layers in the [approved preview](../../../previews/splash-halo.html) as the reference for this revision: a soft upper halo over Navy, with a wide fading rear layer and one brighter curved diagonal ribbon over a narrow shaded fold below. Approval covers the background only, not the example wordmark or layout measurements. Keep the approved preview unchanged; use a new revision for subsequent backdrop variations.

See the [shared static-background pattern](../../patterns/static-background.md) for how to tune halos on other surfaces.
