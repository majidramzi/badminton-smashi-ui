# Static background and adjustable halos

Status: owner-approved direction, 2026-11-20. Applies to web and mobile in the fixed Smashi theme.

- Keep the backdrop static. Halos are decorative design layers, not animations or a user setting.
- Use the [approved brand palette](../../docs/BRAND_LOGO.md) for all color stops and fills. Opacity and blending between those colors are allowed.
- Specify halo position, size, falloff and intensity per screen. Keep decorative bands separate so their shape and overlap can be tuned without changing the halo or content. Keep text and controls readable over the resulting backdrop.
- Use the same approved composition for the same surface on both platforms where applicable. Record any approved adjustments in its screen specification rather than choosing them independently in the apps.
- The [mobile Splash draft](../../previews/splash-halo.html) is the approved backdrop example. Its specific halo and ribbons are not mandatory on other screens.
